HERE TO HELP

Setora support.

Need help with an account, booking or invoice? Contact the Setora team.

Contact us

Email Setora support →

info@idyents.co.uk

Please include your role (agency, venue or DJ), your app version, what you were trying to do and any error message. A booking or invoice reference can help us locate the issue.

Never email passwords, verification codes, full card details or bank statements. Hide unrelated personal information in screenshots.

Bookings and externally arranged payments

For scheduling, DJ arrangements, payment terms or externally arranged refunds, contact your connected agency through Setora. External payment reports are user-reported records; Setora does not verify bank transfers or automatically refund those payments.

Apple subscriptions

Manage or cancel an Apple subscription in your iPhone Settings under your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases on Setora’s Agency plan screen if a purchase is missing. Contact support if access still does not update.

Notifications

Check Setora’s notification preferences and iPhone Settings → Notifications → Setora. Focus modes and device settings may affect when alerts appear.

Privacy and account requests

Review the privacy policy for how information is handled. Account and privacy controls are available in the app; you can also contact support for help with access, correction or deletion requests. Some business and financial records may need to be retained as explained in the policy.

Availability

Setora is preparing for its first public iPhone release. If you are using a TestFlight build, include its build number when reporting an issue.
