This policy covers the Setora app and its related services. Some features, including subscriptions and external-payment tools, may be introduced in stages; the relevant sections explain how information is handled when those features are available to you.

1. Who we are

Setora is a booking and business-management platform connecting DJ agencies, venues and DJs. It supports accounts, recruitment, requests, quotes, event calendars, assignments, messaging, contracts, notifications and payment administration.

Setora is operated by Setora, at 63 Dashwood Avenue, High Wycombe, HP12 3DZ, United Kingdom. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that operator.

Privacy contact: info@idyents.co.uk
General support: info@idyents.co.uk

We handle personal information under the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended, and other applicable UK data-protection and electronic-communications law.

We are responsible as controller for information we use to run, secure and administer Setora. An agency or venue may separately be a controller for its recruitment, event, contractual and business records. Where we process information solely on an organisation’s instructions, our role and obligations must be set out in the applicable data-processing agreement. Contact us if you are unsure which organisation handles a particular request.

2. Information we collect

The information depends on your role and the features you use:

  • Account and identity details: names, email addresses, account identifiers, roles, authentication/recovery information and name-change history. Authentication is handled through Supabase; other agencies or venues cannot view your password.
  • Business and DJ profiles: agency/venue names, addresses and contact details; DJ stage names, biographies, locations, genres, equipment, professional links, rates, availability and marketplace visibility settings.
  • Bookings and events: requests, dates/times, venue details, quotes, amounts/deposits, invoices, DJ offers, acceptances/declines, assignments, contractual records, approvals, changes and cancellations.
  • Communications and uploads: messages, event descriptions, flyers, uploaded photographs and information supplied in support requests or disputes.
  • Payment and subscription records: payment amounts/currencies, fees, payment/refund status, provider references, Stripe-connected-account identifiers and onboarding status; Apple product/transaction identifiers, purchase environment, expiry and revocation information, and an agency-linked purchase token.
  • External payment reports and evidence: invoice responses, reported payments/refunds, references or explanations, who reported them and when, and supporting PDF/image files you choose to upload. These files may contain bank or other personal information.
  • Device and service information: push-notification tokens and environment, notification permissions/preferences and delivery/read state, app settings, access/security/audit records and technical request information, such as IP addresses and timestamps, processed by our infrastructure providers.

We obtain information from you, other authorised users or organisations you work with, our service providers and your use of Setora. For example, an agency may invite you, issue a quote or enter booking information before you open the relevant record.

Please upload only information you are entitled to share. Redact unrelated transactions, full payment-card numbers and unnecessary personal details from payment evidence. Do not use booking notes or messages to submit medical records, identity documents or other sensitive information unrelated to the service.

4. Who can see your information

Authorised agencies, venues, DJs and staff can access the information appropriate to their workspace, role and participation in a booking, event or conversation. DJ profile visibility follows the available marketplace and contact-sharing settings. Creating an account does not automatically make all your information public.

External payment evidence is private storage, not a public link. Before it is attached to an invoice-completion report, the uploader has access to their uploaded file. Once linked, the evidence is available to authorised agency finance readers and venue members with access to that invoice. It is not automatically shared with unrelated agencies, venues or DJs.

Permitted platform administration/support users may access information where necessary for their duties, such as investigating a problem or reviewing an escalated dispute. Agencies and venues remain responsible for their own lawful use and any copies they make.

Our principal service providers include:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, private file storage and backend functions.
  • Stripe: supported booking checkout, connected payment accounts, payment/refund processing and related financial status.
  • Apple: App Store subscription purchasing/verification and Apple Push Notification service delivery on iOS.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this public privacy-policy page. GitHub may process connection/request information, such as IP addresses, when you open it.

These providers may act as processors or independent controllers for different activities. Their own privacy information also applies: Supabase, Stripe and Apple and GitHub.

We may also disclose information to necessary hosting/operations providers, professional advisers, authorities where lawfully required, or a successor in a lawful business transfer, with appropriate protections.

We do not sell personal information. The current native application does not use advertising identifiers, advertising SDKs or cross-app advertising tracking.

5. Payments, subscriptions and external arrangements

Booking payments processed through Stripe: Stripe handles the payment-entry process. Setora receives references, amounts, status and related information needed to administer the booking. Our payment integration does not collect full payment-card numbers or card security codes into Setora’s own payment fields.

Apple agency subscriptions: when enabled, Apple processes the subscription purchase. Setora verifies the product, transaction, agency association, expiry and revocation information to decide whether that agency has access. We do not receive your Apple Account password or full payment-card details. An agency trial is recorded against that agency so that changing a setting does not restart it. Deleting Setora or your Setora account does not itself cancel an Apple subscription; manage it through Apple’s subscription settings.

Payments outside Setora: an agency or venue may report payment completion, upload supporting evidence and record a refund arranged independently. “Reported paid externally” means a user has made that report; it is not confirmation by a bank, Setora or Stripe. Setora does not automatically charge, refund, hold or transfer those external funds. The relevant agency/venue and any external payment provider may separately handle information under their own policies.

We use relevant cancellation, approval and dispute records to administer the supported workflow and prevent inconsistent or duplicate actions. Information submitted in a dispute may be made available to the relevant authorised parties and, where applicable, platform reviewers. This policy is not a replacement for the booking, subscription or cancellation terms.

6. Notifications and information on your device

Setora can send booking, message, account, payment and other service notifications. You can manage the available notification categories in Setora and device permissions in iOS. Depending on your lock-screen settings, notification previews may be visible to someone with access to your device. Necessary account/security communications may still be sent where appropriate.

Sign-in tokens are stored using the device’s protected credential storage. Setora also stores preferences and notification state on the device.

Selected previously loaded bookings, calendars, assignment information and flyers may be stored in an encrypted, account-scoped offline cache. Cached entries are unavailable once they are seven days old and are removed when accessed after expiry; storage limits may remove them sooner. The cache is cleared when the app clears or replaces the relevant local session. Some device preferences and notification identifiers have separate lifecycles.

External payment evidence is not added to this offline-viewing cache. If you explicitly save a quote, invoice, receipt or evidence file to Files, share it or take a screenshot, that separate copy remains under your control and the policies of any storage/sharing service you choose. Removing a Setora record does not erase those copies or copies held lawfully by another party.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, taking account of the record type, active services, legal obligations, disputes and the need to establish or defend claims. We do not apply one universal retention period to every record.

  • Accounts and profiles: kept while needed to operate the account and associated services. Following approved account deletion, sign-in access is removed and relevant profile information is deleted or anonymised, subject to retained records described below.
  • Withdrawn or declined unused quotes: eligible quotes with no booking or checkout/payment links enter a 30-day holding period. Scheduled cleanup then removes the eligible quote content and associated quote detail/history, leaving minimal deletion metadata. This is not an immediate-delete guarantee at the exact deadline.
  • Financially linked quotes, bookings, invoices, reports, evidence and contracts: not covered by that 30-day bin. Records may remain where needed for the contractual relationship, accounts/tax requirements, fraud prevention, disputes or legal claims. Applicable periods depend on the operator, organisation and record type.
  • Messages, notifications, audit, security and support records: retained according to the continuing service, investigation, accountability or legal need for each category. Some notifications and membership information are removed during account closure; closing an account does not necessarily remove shared business records or messages already received by others.
  • Offline cache: follows the seven-day access limit described above. User-exported copies have their own lifecycle.
  • Backups: where provider backups contain deleted information, it may remain in protected backups until the applicable backup cycle expires. If a backup is restored, necessary deletion restrictions must be reapplied.

When deciding whether a record is still needed, we consider whether the booking or contractual relationship is continuing, whether a payment, refund, complaint or dispute remains unresolved, which statutory accounting/tax periods apply, and whether a relevant legal claim can still be brought or must be defended. A legal hold can delay deletion. Contact us for information about the criteria applicable to a particular record.

Hiding a quote from your own list does not delete the shared quote. Restoring a list entry does not reverse another party’s refusal or reactivate a withdrawn financial obligation.

8. Storage and international transfers

Our providers may process information in the UK and other countries. A UK database location alone does not mean that provider support, subprocessors, payments, notifications or backups all stay in the UK.

Where a restricted international transfer is required, the relevant lawful arrangement must apply, such as UK adequacy regulations or appropriate contractual safeguards, including the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum where applicable, with required assessments and supplementary protections. You may contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you can request access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to processing, including where we rely on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent where consent is the basis for the relevant use. Applicable rights concerning solely automated decisions also remain available.

Contact info@idyents.co.uk to make a request. We may ask for information reasonably needed to confirm your identity or understand the request. We normally respond within one month, subject to applicable legal timing rules and permitted extensions; we will explain any extension or restriction. We do not normally charge for exercising these rights, although the law permits charges or refusal in limited circumstances.

You can request account deletion under Account & Privacy in Setora. The app requires reauthentication and confirmation. Active bookings/events or ownership responsibilities may require replacement, cancellation or handover before the normal account-closure workflow can finish safely. You can still make a data-protection request by contacting us, including if you cannot sign in; the app workflow does not remove your statutory rights.

An agency or venue may need to handle a request concerning records for which it is independently responsible. We will explain our role and help identify the appropriate contact where possible.

10. Complaints

Contact info@idyents.co.uk if you are concerned about our use of personal information. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or another competent supervisory authority. You do not have to complete our internal complaint process before contacting the regulator.

11. Security

We use measures including encrypted transport, protected credential storage, role-based access controls, server-side permission checks, private storage, audit records and encrypted offline copies. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep account credentials private and report suspected unauthorised access through info@idyents.co.uk.

12. Age eligibility

Setora is intended for professional DJ, venue and agency operations. You must be 18 or over to create or use a Setora account. Setora is not intended for children under 18. An App Store content age rating does not replace this eligibility rule. If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account or supplied personal information, we will investigate and take appropriate steps to restrict access and remove information where appropriate, subject to any records we must lawfully retain. Please contact info@idyents.co.uk if you have a concern.

13. Planned features and automated checks

The current build does not operate GPS check-in/background location tracking, external-calendar account synchronisation or an enabled third-party AI generation/recommendation service. Availability entries and profile locations entered by users are different from collecting device GPS location.

Before introducing those features, we will explain the new information collected, purpose, relevant provider, sharing, retention, permissions and user controls, and obtain consent where required. Any materially different data use will be reflected in updated notices before collection.

Setora uses automated operational checks, for example scheduling-conflict detection, access permissions, trial/subscription expiry and payment-status reconciliation. An externally reported payment is not automatically bank-verified, and an AI suggestion does not authorise a payment. Contact us if you need an explanation or review of a decision affecting you.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as Setora, our providers or legal requirements change. The published page will show the effective date, and we will give appropriate notice of material changes. Where a new use requires consent, we will request it before relying on that consent.
