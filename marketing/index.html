Clear expectations.

How does access work?

Available tools depend on your role and your agency’s plan. Monthly and annual agency subscription options are shown in the app. Apple subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple subscription settings.

What about external payments?

For externally arranged payments, Setora keeps user-reported records. It does not collect or verify those bank transfers or automatically issue refunds. Agree payment and any settlement directly with your agency.

Do calendar copies update automatically?

No. Adding a booking opens Apple’s calendar editor for you to review and save. Changes and cancellations must be managed in your calendar app.

Is Setora available on the App Store?

Setora is preparing for its first iPhone release. There is no public download yet. Contact us with questions about availability.
